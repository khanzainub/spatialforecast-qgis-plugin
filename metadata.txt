It harmonises mismatched raster grids to a common aligned reference before modelling and produces forecast rasters, diagnostics, statistics and publication-ready graphs. Developed by Zainab Khan and Siddhartha Khare, Geospatial Engineering Group, Department of Civil Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee.
